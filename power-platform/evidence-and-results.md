# Evidence, results, and publication scope
[Power Apps portfolio](README.md)

## Evidence basis

This collection was prepared from the existing public portfolio/profile and available project discussions containing user-supplied application configuration, workflow payloads, errors, and presentation feedback. The earlier portfolio records describe resume-reported scope. Raw resumes and complete solution exports were not re-audited for this collection.

Private conversation links, names, exact timestamps, tenant addresses, identifiers, internal field names, and operational payloads are intentionally excluded.

| Claim | Basis | Limit |

## Results that can be stated

The portfolio demonstrates business-form modernization, typed SharePoint integration, workflow data shaping, and concrete troubleshooting. The engagement app has reported scope and actual control-level evidence. Recognition and tax intake show iterative delivery with acceptance work still outstanding.

No numerical time saving, adoption rate, service-level improvement, flawless production operation, or completed business acceptance is inferred. Document generation, signing, or client delivery in the engagement app is not established.

Future outcome reporting should use an agreed baseline and measurement period: request-to-assignment time, missing-field rate, failed-notification rate, duplicate output count, and user acceptance. These are proposed measures, not measured results.

## Sanitization and screenshots

Only generic roles, example column/control names, and neutral business descriptions are published. Formulas were written as standalone illustrations of common platform patterns. No proprietary app export, solution ZIP, client document, actual employee message, tenant URL, live identifier, email address, or internal destination is included in the new case studies.

No screenshot in the reviewed material was established as both available and safe to publish. Consequently this collection contains text and generalized Mermaid diagrams only. It does not present recreated images as product screenshots.

## Documentation validation

Before publication, check relative links, Markdown structure, example labels, unsupported success claims, identifiers, contact details, and private endpoint patterns. Documentation verification is distinct from executing the app/flows or completing UAT.
